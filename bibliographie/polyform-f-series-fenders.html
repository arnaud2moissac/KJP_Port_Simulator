F-series

Heavy duty fenders

The Polyform F-series cylindrical fenders have set the standard for heavy duty fenders for over 60 years. Polyform's unique, in-house developed and patented POLYMATIQ® technology ensures supreme control over the fusion process and warrants for the most consistent quality available.

F-series cylindrical fenders are offered in 12 different sizes to fit vessels ranging from the smallest dinghy up to commercial ships and Navy vessels. More info in our fender-guide here.

The POLYFORM F-series boat fenders are synonymous with quality, easily identified by their iconic blue, rib-reinforced rope holds.

Key features:
  • Extra flexible body material for superior shock absorption.
  • Sturdy, uniform wall thickness ensures no splits, cracks or weak points.
  • UV resistant and cold flex plastic suitable for all climates and conditions.
  • Ribbed and reinforced, high-pressure injection molded solid rope hold for exceptional strength and for added durability.
  • All plastic valves integrated through friction welding  - no glue and no corrosion
  • Halv a turn screw, integrated non-return flap, and unique adapter tool for easy inflation and air pressure adjustment.
  • Perfectly seamless construction and smooth surface and glossy finish for a premium appearance.

 

 

The fenders are tested for strength and flexibility in temperatures ranging from -30oC to +50oC, and feature high abrasive resistance and high energy absorption (up to 3.8 ton meter for F-13) making them suitable for ships of up to 1500 ton d/w (F-13).

Find inflation guide and useful info here.

Reducing Footprint

In line with our commitment to sustainability, Polyform includes bio-sourced* raw materials in all our inflatable buoys and fenders, reducing carbon footprint by 8%.

The same unique quality with an improved environmental footprint.

*Bio-attributed materials substitute fossil feedstock with renewable biomass under the principles of mass balance.
